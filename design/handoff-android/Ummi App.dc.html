1Ummi for Android: working prototype
Both phones are fully clickable. Tick acts and set reminders on Today, tap a week or Month 6 to jump to Journey, and tap Read to open the text. Add a visit with the button on Visits. Names, reflections and the theme switch are on More.
Content comes from the repo's data: weeks, months, food, duas and the Qurʾān. Prayer times, visits and names are sample data.
